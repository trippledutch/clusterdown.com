Privacy statement

Version 1, 27 November 2026. Applies to clustertriage.com, clusterdown.com and portal.clustertriage.com.

Who we are

ClusterTriage and ClusterDown are run by Hans Vredevoort, who works as a sole trader from Thailand. There is no registered company. For anything about your data, write to support@clustertriage.com.

For the data in your own account and on our websites we decide what is done with it (we are the controller). For the measurement data your organisation uploads, your organisation decides and we process it on its behalf; the data processing agreement covers that part.

Transfers outside the European Economic Area rely on the safeguards each provider offers, such as the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

How we protect it

Uploads are encrypted on your machine for our key before they leave it. On the server the data sits on an encrypted disk, sensitive fields are encrypted again, every customer is locked off from the others in the database, and staff sign in with a passkey. The details are on How we handle credentials.

Your rights

You may ask to see, correct, export or delete your data, object to its use, or ask us to limit it. Write to support@clustertriage.com; we answer within a month. For measurement data, ask your own organisation first, as it decides about that data. You may also complain to the data protection authority of the country where you live or work.

Changes

When this statement changes, the new version is published here with its date. Account holders are told by e-mail about changes that matter.
